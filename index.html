Field Reports

Turns out I don't want that new pair of lululemons.

Backpacking

Everything in a bag.
That's more than enough.

There's a specific kind of liberation that comes from reducing your entire life into a backpack. I was happiest when that was all I had with me. But I was also admiring mountains and tanning on the beach of Croatia with 2 Canadian girls. I met them a day before. We went on to talk about the lessons we'd learnt from our past heartbreaks.

The best conversations are sometimes with those you meet once and never again.

Worldpackers

Working my way through the world.

This was more than just work. Not work at all actually. We lived in a 10-bed dorm together. Talked, laughed, cooked, ate, shopped, partied together. This would usually be a nightmare, but they were some of the most genuine and kind people I know. So that's ok.

Highly recommend. You'll come back a new person.

Love and miss you guys so much xx

"Riding a camel at sunrise was insaaaane."

The desert is vast and isolated. Being in it makes you wonder how much beauty there is in the world that we haven't seen. And felt.
