Not a résumé.
My life in print.

I'm Kaie. I've got a degree in something I don't currently use, I've unintentionally gone on a gap year, and I'm still navigating my career path. But that's alright, I'm convinced there's much more to living than what we are influenced to think.

This page is less about what I've done on paper and more about who I am when the paper runs out.

Travel & Experiences

Field Reports

Carrying everything you need on your back changes the way you think about need.

Backpacking

Everything fits in a bag.
And that's more than enough.

There's a specific kind of freedom that comes from reducing your entire life into a backpack. I was happiest when that was all I had with me. But I was also admiring mountains and tanning on the beach of Croatia with 2 Canadian girls. I met them a day before. We went on to talk about the lessons we'd learnt from our past heartbreaks.

The best conversations are sometimes with those you see once and never again.

Worldpackers

Working my way through the world.

This was more than just work. Not work at all actually. We lived in a 10-bed dorm together. Talked, laughed, cooked, ate, shopped, partied together. Usually this would be a nightmare, but they were some of the most genuine and kind people I know. So that's ok.

Highly recommend. You'll come back a new person.

The places you earn feel different from the ones you pay for.

"Riding a camel at sunrise is exactly as absurd and magnificent as it sounds."

Some experiences exist outside of language. The desert does that to you - it's vast, isolated, and makes you wonder how much beauty there is in the world that we haven't seen. And felt.
