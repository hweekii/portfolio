Backpacking

Everything in a bag.
That's more than enough.

My first backpacking trip! I had little with me, but experienced so much. Had a few bad turns here and there, but I finally got to see the Dolomites. I teared up at the start of my first hike (I had dreamt about it for months beforehand). Later on I found myself tanning on the beach of Croatia with 2 Canadian girls. I met them a day before. We went on to talk about the lessons we'd learnt from our past heartbreaks.

The best conversations are sometimes with those you meet once and never again.

Worldpackers

Introvert Volunteering in a Hostel

This was a rollercoaster ride. I learnt so many lessons, good and horrendously bad ones. We lived in a 10-bed dorm together. Talked, laughed, cooked, ate, shopped, partied together. This would usually be a nightmare, but they were some of the most genuine and kind people I know. So that's ok.

Also got exposed to techno. Wasn't a huge fan.

Love and miss you guys so much xx

Bring me back to Morocco!!

"Wow."

You could look into the far distance and see no end to the desert. Somehow the guides knew exactly where they were going. At -1°C on our camels, we saw the sun go from navy to dusk to yellow to pink. Being in it makes you wonder how much beauty there is in the world that we haven't felt.
